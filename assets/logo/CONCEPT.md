# The FORMORA mark

Made with FORMORA's own method: one concept, two visual elements, one unified symbol.

- **One concept:** a letter that becomes a creature, which is what the skill does.
- **Two elements:** the capital F of FORMORA, and a falcon's head in profile.
- **One symbol:** the F's top arm ends in a hooked beak, its lower arm is cut on the same slope as a feather,
  and the eye is a circle cut out of the corner where stem and arm meet.

## Directions considered

| # | Direction | Technique | Score /100 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Falcon F** — the F's arm is a beak, the eye is cut out | Letterform morphing + negative space | 84 | Chosen: reads as a letter first, a bird second |
| 02 | Two circles overlapping, with an F in the shared area | Negative-space fusion | 72 | Says "two into one" but the F is weak at small sizes |
| 03 | F with a pen-nib stem | Typography illustration | 66 | Generic "design tool" image |
| 04 | F built from a square and a circle | Symbolic geometry | 70 | Clean but carries no second meaning |
| 05 | Square block with an F and a bird cut out of it | Hidden symbol | 68 | Too many cuts; breaks down at 32 px |

## Construction

512 grid in 32-unit steps. One merged outline plus one even-odd cut-out for the eye; both sloped ends share the
same angle. Checked in `preview.html`: the F holds at 16 px, the eye is visible from 32 px.
