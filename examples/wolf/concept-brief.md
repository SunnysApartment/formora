## Selected direction and rationale

- **Selected:** 02 Wolf Mask W
- **Chosen by:** the assistant (the test brief said to choose)
- **Rationale:** the only direction where letter and animal are one shape with nothing added, and the only one that scores full marks for small sizes.
- **Construction:** one closed 14-point outline on a 512 grid. Outer top corners pulled up into ear points; outer edges taper inward toward the feet to suggest a muzzle; two slanted triangular eye cut-outs made with even-odd fill, mirrored about x = 256.
- **Refinement made after first render:** widened the cheeks and tapered the feet inward (v1 read as a plain W with eyes).

## Image prompt (not run — test stopped short of spending image credits)

Create a professional conceptual logo for WOLF, a brand in outdoor equipment.
CORE CONCEPT: Combine the capital letter W and a front-facing wolf head into one unified visual mark.
VISUAL FUSION: The W is the wolf's face. The two outer top corners of the W extend upward into pointed ears; the two outer strokes are the cheeks, tapering inward toward the bottom; two slanted triangular cut-outs in the outer strokes are the eyes. Nothing is added beside the letter.
TYPOGRAPHY: A single heavy geometric capital W, clearly readable as a W. No other text.
CONSTRUCTION: Symmetrical, straight edges only, solid filled shape, eyes as negative space.
STYLE: Flat, bold, geometric. COLOR: Black on white only. BACKGROUND: Clean, plain white.
RESTRICTIONS: No separate icon beside text, no gradients, shadows, 3D, mockups or extra symbols.
OUTPUT: One centred logo symbol.
