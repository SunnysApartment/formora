## Letter analysis (done before any concept)

Read right to left. One connected group, no break:

| Letter | Form in this word | Shape | Dots |
|---|---|---|---|
| ص sad | initial (joins left) | closed loop followed by a small tooth | none |

No vertical strokes rise above the loops, so the word is low and wide.

## Selected direction and rationale

- **Selected:** 01 Kufi Profile. **Chosen by:** the assistant (test run).
- **Rationale:** square Kufi sits on a grid, so it can be hand-built as real paths, and it is the only direction where the whole word carries the bird.
- **Construction:** 32-unit grid, one stroke weight of one unit, one merged outline with two even-odd counters and two square dots. ر ends in a pointed hook; the right side of ص slants to a point.
- **Refinements after rendering:** shortened the ر descender to match the proportion of a reference Kufi face; merged all touching shapes into one outline after hairline seams appeared between them.

## Status

- Letter structure compared by eye against the same word set in two system Arabic fonts (see `compare.png`): loop + tooth, loop + two dots, descender all match.
- **Not validated by a native Arabic reader.** The skill requires that test before approval.
- Version 1 (`logo/wordmark-v1.svg`): falcon reading weak.
- Version 2 (`logo/wordmark.svg`): ر redrawn as a hooked beak wedge, ق given a chamfered crown and diamond dots, ص loop lifted into a wing with a three-feather tip. Letter count, joins, dot count and position unchanged.
- Risk in version 2: ر is now a slanted wedge rather than a plain descending stroke, and the feather notches are new to the ص outline. Both need the native-reader test.
